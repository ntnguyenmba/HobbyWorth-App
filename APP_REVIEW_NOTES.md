# HobbyWorth App Review notes

## Category
Productivity or Lifestyle

## Subtitle options
- First project planner
- Track cost and time
- See if a batch is worth it

## Description opening
Plan one first project. Save cost, time, and how many you made on this device. See leftover, per unit, and per hour at a price you type. Optional one-time unlock to keep history and compare batches.

## App Review information
This app helps someone complete one first project and record cost, time, yield, and an asking price. It calculates leftover, per-unit, and per-hour figures from those inputs. It does not find customers, process payments, or guarantee income. Projects stay on device. No account required. IAP unlocks history, comparison, cost breakdown, and export.

## Store language guardrails
Do not use: easy income, start earning today, side hustle that pays, your hobby will make money.

Allowed supporting sentence: See whether a batch could cover its cost if you chose to sell it.
